Business knowledge + e-commerce specification + verified source information

0. Purpose

This is the master information/specification document for an e-commerce website built around Cake Box, Kakinada. It is designed to give the development team the business facts, product structure, customer-facing information, and system requirements needed for the website.

Rule: information not exposed by the supplied sources is marked UNKNOWN / NOT VERIFIED. Do not invent prices, delivery rules, policies, or services.

5. Online ordering

Zomato provides an Order Online page for Cake Box.

Zomato states that online ordering is supported through its mobile app.

At retrieval, Zomato displayed 'Currently closed for online ordering'. This is a time-dependent status.

Zomato showed live-order/live-tracking functionality and an approximately 30-minute indicator on the current listing.

For the project's own website, online ordering should be controlled through admin settings.

28. Data validation rules

Never invent a price.

Never assume eggless status from a product name.

Never assume delivery zones or fees.

Keep third-party ratings separate from native website ratings.

Preserve original product names and keep normalized names separately.

Use INR for this branch unless expansion requires other currencies.

Separate product existence from current availability.

Separate store hours from online-ordering hours.

Timestamp prices, availability, ratings and hours.

Keep source/provenance for important facts.

32. Final implementation recommendation

Use this document as the business/e-commerce specification and keep the separate product/menu dataset as the import source for the product database. Build the application so administrators can update prices, availability, hours, offers, delivery rules and policies without changing code. Keep source and verification timestamps for volatile information.
